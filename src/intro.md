# Welcome to Earthmania

**Earthmania** is a growing, connected knowledge base on how materials, industrial
processes and systems actually work — from the bottle in your hand to the
chemistry that makes it, the machines that process it, and where it ends up.

This is **not** an academic text, but it takes accuracy seriously. Pages explain
the mechanics in plain language, define their terms, and are improved over time.

## How this guide works

Earthmania is a connected map rather than a list of articles. Each page explains
one material, process, impact or system: what it physically is, what it turns
into, which other systems it touches, and where the real technical limits sit.
Links between pages are the point. Follow them from a plastic bottle to the
chemistry that makes it, the machines that recycle it, and the places it ends up.

There are no verdicts like "X is bad, use Y". The aim is to explain how things
actually work, well enough that you can draw your own conclusions and act on them.

## Our approach

Earthmania grows slowly on purpose. The goal is a small but solid knowledge base
that you can trust, not a huge one built quickly. Every page is written by a human
and checked claim by claim against its sources before it is published. Numbers
always come with their region, year and source, and where experts disagree, we
show the range instead of picking one.

We are part of a wider world of open knowledge. Where others already do something
well, such as [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org) for data and charts,
we link to them rather than repeat them.

Spotted an error or know a better source? Please
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/buerlino/earthmania/issues).
